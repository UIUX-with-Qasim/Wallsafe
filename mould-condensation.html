The Problem

What Is It &
Why Does It Matter?

Mould and condensation are the most misdiagnosed damp problems in UK homes. Bleach kills mould temporarily but does nothing about the moisture causing it. The result? It always returns. We find and fix the root cause.

Warning Signs to Watch For

Black or green mould on walls, ceilings or window frames

Condensation regularly forming on windows or cold walls

Persistent musty smell especially in bedrooms

Mould returning after cleaning or painting over

Family members with worsening respiratory issues

Cold spots on internal walls especially in corners

How We Fix It

Questions

Common Questions
About Mould & Condensation

Why does my mould keep coming back?
+

Because the moisture source hasn't been addressed. Bleach kills spores but nothing about the humidity or poor ventilation causing growth. We fix the cause, not the symptom.

Is black mould dangerous?
+

It can be. Mould spores aggravate asthma and cause respiratory problems — particularly in children and the elderly. Take it seriously.

Is mould always structural damp?
+

No. It can be condensation from cooking or bathing in a poorly ventilated property, or external water ingress. Our assessment identifies which.

Can you treat rented properties?
+

Yes. We work with both tenants and landlords. We recommend informing your landlord before booking.
